2. **Scene 2:** `[MASTER] Close-up of YU's hands holding up the oatmeal open-knit sweater above the open box on the cream bed, pulling the thin waist tie so the knit pattern and belt are clearly visible. 50mm, shallow depth of field.`
3. **Scene 3:** `[MASTER] YU stands in front of a tall standing mirror in the same bedroom, wearing the full set (sweater with the belt tied at the waist, black pleated skirt), tying the belt, skirt pleats swinging slightly. Medium-full mirror shot.`
4. **Scene 4:** `[MASTER] YU near a bright window, lifting the cream ruched shoulder bag by its scrunchie handle and placing it on her shoulder, smiling. Close-up on the bag and upper body, shallow depth of field.`
5. **Scene 5:** `[MASTER] Sidewalk in front of a small café with autumn leaves on the ground, golden late-afternoon light. YU walks toward the camera in the full set with the shoulder bag, stops and does a half-turn so the pleated skirt flares. Full-body shot.`

## Prompt video (image-to-video per keyframe, 3 detik)
Gunakan keyframe scene sebagai start frame, lalu tambahkan:
- S1: `She opens the box flaps and looks up at the camera, saying in Indonesian: "Paket outfit fall aku dateng!" Handheld micro-shake.`
- S2: `Hands slowly lift and spread the sweater, then tug the waist tie; slow tilt down. Voiceover: "Sweater rajut, ada tali pinggangnya."`
- S3: `She finishes tying the belt and turns 3/4 to the mirror; skirt pleats move naturally. She says: "Plus rok lipit hitam, langsung rapi."`
- S4: `She slides the bag onto her shoulder and adjusts the scrunchie handle. She says: "Tasnya kerut-kerut, lucu banget."`

Larangan untuk semua scene: wajah dan outfit harus konsisten, tangan dan jari normal, pola rajutan tidak berubah, kain tidak tembus pandang, tidak ada teks buatan model.
